# AI assistance in AI702 labs

This policy applies to assessed implementation, experiments, transfer tasks and explanations.
Authorized teaching staff may implement reference solutions and maintain this infrastructure.
Staff solutions and hidden tests must never be included in a student release.

## Assessed work

During assessed core, transfer and controlled individual work, conversational AI,
generative code completion and autonomous agents are prohibited. This includes asking
AI to debug, review, rewrite or generate any part of an assessed answer, and running
an edit-test-fix loop. Disable AI assistants and completion extensions in the course
environment. Do not delegate work to another person or fabricate evidence.

Use the course handouts, supplied documentation, public tests and TA help allowed for
that session. The TA may explain a concept or error and suggest a diagnostic step.
The student writes the implementation and explanation. TA help is recorded in notes.
Assessment supervision, not an agent instruction file, enforces this resource boundary.

## Preparation outside assessed work

Conceptual AI study before an assessed session is permitted. Do not upload the lab,
request its solution, obtain task-specific code, or prepare generated explanations
for submission. Record relevant permitted preparation in notes. If assistance crossed
the boundary, disclose it to the teaching team before submitting, rather than conceal it.

## Evidence and review

Only student.py, settings.json and notes.md are normally editable. The signed Lab 4
late task additionally permits repair.py. Do not modify tests, runners, shared helpers,
policy, manifests, evidence or grading controls. Settings must match the staff-approved
assignment. Core and transfer are captured separately through the LMS.

The teaching team uses trusted checks, raw evidence, signed late conditions and
controlled individual exercises. Tests and local hashes do not establish authorship.
Unexpected results require human review and the university's normal process. No
personal-history scanning or AI-detector score is used as proof of misconduct.

Read the published assessment arrangements before starting. Contact the teaching
team about approved accessibility or supervised make-up arrangements.
